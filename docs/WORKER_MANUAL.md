# Greena Worker Manual

For farm workers (`farm_worker`). Your job in Greena is fast, accurate daily
data entry. This is short on purpose.

## What you can do
- **Log daily production**: eggs, milk, or other output for your houses/flocks.
- **Log feed**: feed given / consumed.
- **Log weigh-ins**: weights for growth tracking.
- **Record mortality**: deaths, with cause where known.
- **Log health events**: vaccinations given and health observations (varies by farm setup).
- **View** the animals and tasks assigned to you.

You **cannot** change finance, team members, billing, or farm settings — that's for managers and owners. If something needs changing there, tell your manager.

## Your daily routine
1. **Open Greena** on your phone or computer and log in.
2. **Check today's tasks** (Operations Planner materialises them each morning).
3. **Record as you work** — don't wait until the end of the day; log feed, production, and any deaths when they happen.
4. **Vaccinations**: you'll see reminders for **upcoming** and **overdue** vaccinations — do them and mark them done.
5. **End of day**: make sure the day's log is complete. If nothing is logged by 20:00, the farm gets a reminder — beat it.

## Tips for good data
- **Log at the source** — count now, enter now. Guessed-later numbers hurt the farm's reports.
- **Right house/flock** — double-check you're entering against the correct one.
- **Deaths matter** — always record mortality; it drives health alerts and honest performance.
- **Ask ARIA** — you can ask the assistant simple questions like "what do I feed house 2 today?" if your farm allows it.

## Getting help
- Something looks wrong or you can't enter data → tell your farm manager.
- Forgot your password → use "forgot password" on the login screen; never share your password with anyone.
